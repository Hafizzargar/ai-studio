# A short 3D cartoon conversation. Stage directions are rendered as gestures.

Pip [think, curious]: Why does a sunny day make my puddle smaller?
Maya [point, happy]: The Sun warms the water, and some of it evaporates into invisible water vapor.
Ravi [wave, happy]: High in the cool sky, the vapor condenses into tiny droplets that make clouds.
Pip [point, surprised]: Then rain falls when those droplets grow heavy?
Maya [celebrate, happy]: That's right! Rain is precipitation, and the water flows back to rivers and oceans.
Ravi [wave, happy]: The Sun warms it again, and the water cycle starts over!
